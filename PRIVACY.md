Privacy Policy (Draft)

ResQconnect (Aegis Treasury) is committed to protecting your privacy. This is a starter template — please review with legal counsel before publishing.

1. Data collected
- Account identifiers and billing metadata via GitHub Marketplace
- Customer-provided contact and organization information
- Audit logs necessary for security and troubleshooting
- If you use our SaaS custody features, limited transaction metadata may be stored to provide services

2. How we use data
- Provision and manage subscriptions and licenses
- Provide the Aegis Treasury service and customer support
- Compliance with legal obligations

3. Data retention
- Customer data is retained for the duration of the customer's subscription plus 90 days by default. Specific retention schedules can be negotiated for enterprise plans.

4. Security
- We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Keys are protected via HSM/KMS when under our custody.
